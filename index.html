Kshetrimayum Lochan

Project Assistant Professor at Kobe University.

I am a Project Assistant Professor in the Graduate School of System Informatics at Kobe University, Japan, working in the CINAPS Lab. My research focuses on robot dynamics and control, autonomous robotic manipulation, perception, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.

My research trajectory began with modelling and robust control of flexible robotic manipulators and later expanded to nonlinear and adaptive control, underwater manipulation, visual servoing, agricultural robotics, and learning-based autonomous manipulation. My current work studies how vision, language, perception, policy inference, and robot control can be integrated for real-world language-conditioned manipulation.

I am particularly interested in combining nonlinear and robust control with Vision-Language-Action models and embodied intelligence to develop reliable robotic systems for complex and uncertain environments.
