Kshetrimayum Lochan

Project Assistant Professor at Kobe University, Japan

I am a robotics researcher at the CINAPS Lab, Graduate School of System Informatics. My work spans robot dynamics and control, autonomous robotic manipulation, perception, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.

My research began with robust and nonlinear control of flexible manipulators and has expanded through underwater manipulation, visual servoing and agricultural robotics toward learning-based manipulation and embodied AI. My current research integrates vision, language, perception, policy inference and robot control for real-world language-conditioned manipulation.

Email · Google Scholar · Scopus · GitHub

research vision

My long-term goal is to develop robust, adaptive and intelligent robotic systems that operate reliably in complex and uncertain real-world environments. I am particularly interested in combining model-based nonlinear control with multimodal perception, robot learning, VLA policies and embodied intelligence.
