Kshetrimayum Lochan

Project Assistant Professor at Kobe University.

I am a Project Assistant Professor in the Graduate School of System Informatics at Kobe University, Japan, working in the CINAPS Lab. My research focuses on robotic manipulation, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, robot dynamics and control, and embodied AI.

My research trajectory began with modelling and robust control of flexible robotic manipulators and subsequently expanded to nonlinear and adaptive control, underwater robotic manipulation, visual servoing, agricultural robotics, and learning-based autonomous manipulation.

My current work at Kobe University investigates how vision, language, perception, policy inference, and robot control can be integrated to enable manipulators to execute autonomous tasks from natural-language instructions.

I am particularly interested in combining model-based nonlinear control with VLA models, multimodal perception, and embodied intelligence to build robust robotic systems for complex real-world environments.
